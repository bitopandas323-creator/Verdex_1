Last updated: August 20, 2026

Privacy Policy

What we collect

If you just search or browse (Address search, Browse areas, Map view): nothing personal tied to your identity. When you type an address, that search text is sent to OpenStreetMap's free geocoding service (Nominatim) to resolve it to a location, and to environmental data providers (WAQI for air quality, Sentinel Hub for satellite green cover, Open-Meteo for weather) to fetch real data for that location — none of these services receive your name, contact info, or any other identifying detail, just the search text itself.

If you post a listing on Find a Place: the listing title, price, address, the tags you choose, and any photos you upload are stored in our database (hosted on Supabase). Your contact method (phone/email/WhatsApp) is stored in a separate, restricted table that cannot be read directly by anyone browsing the site — it's only revealed to someone else after they explicitly tap "I'm interested."

When someone reveals a listing's contact info: we log a one-way, non-reversible hash of their IP address (not the actual IP, and never linked to any other identifying information) — this exists purely to prevent spam/abuse of the reveal feature, and is never used to identify or track anyone.

If you rate an area's safety: your rating is stored completely anonymously (the same hashed-IP mechanism above is used only to prevent spam, never to identify you).

If you save a shortlist, set a personalized profile, or complete onboarding: this is stored using your browser's local storage only — it never leaves your device or reaches our servers.

How your contact information is protected

Your phone/email is stored in a separate, restricted part of our database that cannot be read directly, even by us, except through the one specific action that reveals it to someone who taps "I'm interested" on your listing. This is a real technical safeguard, not just a setting we could change — your contact details are never included in any public listing data.

Managing or deleting your data

Since Verdex doesn't use accounts, you manage your own listing through a private link generated when you post it — you can edit or delete your listing yourself at any time using that link. If you've lost that link, or want any other data removed, contact us at bitopandas323@gmail.com and we'll help.

Who else sees your data

We don't sell or share your data with advertisers or third parties for marketing. We use Supabase to host our database and store listing photos, and Resend to deliver any email notifications you sign up for — these providers process data only to provide their service to us, not for their own purposes. Address searches pass through OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service and our environmental data providers (WAQI, Sentinel Hub, Open-Meteo), as described above.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, you have the right to know what data we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted. Since Verdex is built without accounts, most of this is already self-service (your private listing link). For anything else, contact bitopandas323@gmail.com.

Terms of Use

What Verdex is, and isn't

Verdex is a platform for finding neighbourhood information and connecting people around housing — it is not a party to any rental, lease, or subletting agreement between users. Any agreement you make with another user is between you and them.

About listings

Listings on Find a Place are posted directly by users. We do not verify the identity of posters or the accuracy of listing details. Use your own judgment, and treat online listings with the same care you would anywhere else — verify details yourself before making any commitment or payment. Use the Report function if you encounter a listing that seems fake, inappropriate, or fraudulent.

About the environmental and area data

Every score is clearly labeled as either "exact for this point" (measured directly from satellite or map data at that specific coordinate) or an "area estimate" (a broader estimate for the general neighbourhood). We aim for accuracy but this data is provided for informational purposes and should not be the sole basis for any major decision.

Prohibited conduct

Don't post fake listings, harass other users, or attempt to circumvent the platform's privacy protections (for example, scraping contact information). Violations may result in your listing being removed.

Limitation of liability

Verdex is provided as-is. We work to keep data accurate and the platform secure, but we can't guarantee the accuracy of user-submitted listings or be responsible for agreements made between users.
