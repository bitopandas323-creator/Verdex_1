Why we exist

The things that actually shape daily life in a neighbourhood — air quality, how much green cover it has, water quality, heat, noise, safety, and how walkable and well-connected it is — are almost never visible until you've already moved in. Verdex blends nine of those real, measurable factors into one live score for any address, computed the same way every time, so you can see it before you sign a lease, not after.
